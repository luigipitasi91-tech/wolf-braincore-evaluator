# Final competition video script — WOLF AI Change Gate

Target: **about 1 minute 55 seconds**.
Do not exceed 3 minutes.

## 0:00–0:12
### Screen
Open:
https://wolf-braincore-evaluator.onrender.com/?demo=1

### Voice
“AI builders change prompts, skills and planning logic constantly. A change can improve one answer while silently losing a constraint or weakening verification. Too often, it still gets promoted because it looks smarter.”

## 0:12–0:24
### Screen
Point briefly at:
**Should this AI change be promoted?**

The canonical request is already loaded. Press **W**.

### Voice
“WOLF is a pre-release change gate for AI builders. It gives the baseline and candidate the exact same request, the same rubric, and the same hard blockers.”

## 0:24–0:43
### Screen
Show the verdict panel:
- PROMOTE
- Baseline 49/100
- Candidate 94/100
- Delta +45
- Request Integrity CLEAR

### Voice
“Here the candidate improves from 49 to 94, clears the required delta, and leaves no critical integrity or verification blocker. WOLF promotes the change.”

## 0:43–1:02
### Screen
Scroll only enough to show the five bars.

### Voice
“Both plans are scored with identical rules: ambiguity resolution, assumption exposure, constraint retention, definition of done, and verification readiness. Longer text does not automatically win.”

## 1:02–1:20
### Screen
Briefly show the two plans. Expand or leave visible:
- Unknowns
- Definition of done
- Verification

Then show the SHA-256 receipt.

### Voice
“The decision stays inspectable. WOLF exposes what the candidate clarified and how success would be verified, then hashes the evaluated evidence into a SHA-256 receipt.”

## 1:20–1:45
### Screen
Click **Run benchmark**.

Pause on:
- Under-specified intent → HOLD
- Conflicting constraints → HOLD
- Short but specific → PROMOTE

### Voice
“This is not a cherry-picked demo. The fixed benchmark also tests failure. WOLF holds vague or contradictory requests, but it does not punish a request merely for being short.”

## 1:45–1:55
### Screen
Return visually to the WOLF identity or leave the benchmark visible.

### Voice
“WOLF judges the change, not the brand of model that produced it. Candidate output is not the same thing as verified improvement.”

## Recording checklist
- Record the real live Render app.
- Browser zoom: 100%.
- Desktop width around 1440px if possible.
- Close unrelated tabs.
- Disable notifications.
- No private data.
- No copyrighted music.
- Speak slowly enough that the scores remain readable.
- Do not show Na0mi, trading, Market Lens, Adamo, or unrelated WOLF experiments.
- Export in 1080p if available.
- Upload to YouTube or Vimeo as a publicly visible video, in line with the official submission rules.
